Ongoing bereavement support

After the Funeral

Grief does not follow a timetable, and care should not necessarily end on the day of the funeral.

This three-month programme offers individual follow-up conversations, compassionate listening, spiritual encouragement, prayer and Scripture where welcomed, and agreed follow-up contact.

This is spiritual bereavement care, not clinical grief counselling, psychotherapy or medical treatment. Referral guidance can be discussed when additional professional support is needed. Participation is voluntary and based on individual consent.

A personal service rooted in faith

I Am Ruth

I am Ruth Wa Boaz, and my work is rooted in my Christian faith and my desire to serve people with dignity, compassion and thoughtful care.

I offer wedding and funeral officiating, chaplaincy and spiritual care. My particular focus is walking alongside people through significant moments in life — moments of joy, commitment and new beginnings, as well as moments of loss, remembrance and grief.

Whether I am preparing a ceremony or offering spiritual care, I begin by listening, understanding your wishes and treating every person with respect.

My desire is to reflect the character and values of Jesus through practical, personal service.
